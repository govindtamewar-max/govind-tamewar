Profile

I am a Journalism and Mass Communication student at Chandigarh University, with roots in Basmath, Maharashtra. My work sits at the intersection of digital media, public relations, and civic journalism — driven by the belief that quality reporting and strategic communication are tools for real social change.

I founded and run CIVICLENS.IN, a digital news platform covering national politics, geopolitics, and business. I lead the editorial strategy, manage a cross-functional team, and drive platform growth through SEO, content marketing, and youth-focused reporting.

My journalism training is complemented by a prior Diploma in Pharmacy and hands-on NGO fieldwork through Malhar Foundation — giving me an unusually grounded perspective on research, community impact, and evidence-based storytelling.
